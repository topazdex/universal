# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Argue about code, not about people.

## What that means here

This is a technical project and most disagreement in it is technical. Technical disagreement is
welcome — it is how the routing path got the numbers it has. Say what you think is wrong and why,
bring evidence, and let the measurement settle it.

Expected:

- Critique the change, not the person who wrote it.
- Assume the other person read the same code you did and reached a different conclusion honestly.
- Be direct. "This loses 30 bips on split trades, here is the run" is kind. Vagueness to avoid
  bluntness wastes everyone's time.
- Take a maintainer's "no" without relitigating it three times. Some invariants exist because
  breaking them already cost someone a week.

Not accepted:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests or anywhere else this project
  operates.
- Discrimination or demeaning comments based on who someone is.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption: brigading, bad-faith argument, spam.

This applies to everyone — contributors, maintainers, and anyone who shows up in a thread.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and pull requests that do not
belong here, and will say why. Repeated or severe behaviour gets a ban from the repository.

To report something, use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on the
[Security tab](https://github.com/topazdex/universal/security/advisories/new) — it is a private
channel to the maintainers and works for conduct reports as well as for vulnerabilities. Reports are
handled discreetly, and the person reporting is not identified to the person reported.
